How the Platform Works

A streamlined 4-step governance workflow from creation to certificate issuance.

1

Event Creation

Faculty submit event proposals specifying department, venue, schedule, and participant limits.

2

Approval & Registration

Once approved by HOD/Dean, events open for instant online registration across the student portal.

3

Live Attendance & Scoring

Event coordinators capture live attendance and assign performance marks during the session.

4

Certificates & Feedback

Attended students submit feedback and automatically unlock official downloadable certificates.
